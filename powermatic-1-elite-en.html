About the Powermatic I+ Elite

The Zorr Powermatic I+ Elite is the classic manual solution for anyone looking for a robust, durable and low-maintenance rolling machine. Its sturdy mechanics and simple operation make it especially attractive for beginners and for users who deliberately want to do without electric technology.
