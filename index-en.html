Maintenance & Care

For consistently strong results.

Regular cleaning and proper use significantly extend the lifespan of your Zorr Powermatic rolling machine.

Maintenance and Care

To keep your ZORR® Powermatic rolling machine working reliably over the long term, regular cleaning and careful use are recommended. During the rolling process, tobacco residue can accumulate in the filling channel or inside the device.

We therefore recommend regularly clearing tobacco residue from the machine with a small brush or a suitable cleaning brush. The filling channel and moving parts in particular should be kept clean. Also make sure to loosen the tobacco slightly before use rather than packing it too tightly. Tobacco that is too dry or too densely packed can make the rolling process harder.

Gentle handling also contributes to the device's longevity. Avoid excessive force and use the machine only as intended. For electric models, it is also recommended to regularly disconnect the device from power and clear it of dust and tobacco particles.

Tips for a Long Lifespan

Clean regularly: Remove tobacco residue from the chamber and filling area.
Use cleaning tools: Keep the filling channel and mechanics regularly clean.
Remove tobacco dust: Clear small particles with a brush or compressed air.
Don't overfill: Too much tobacco can cause clogging and strain the mechanics.

* Only clean the device while switched off. Always switch off electric devices before cleaning and do not reach into the filling area.
